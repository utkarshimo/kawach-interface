FLOODS 101

What is a flood, and why India?

A flood is water covering land that is normally dry. It happens when rain, rivers, the sea or a broken dam deliver more water than the land can carry away. India's monsoon rain, huge river basins and crowded plains make it one of the most flood-exposed countries.

Types of floods

🌊River (riverine)

Rivers overflow after long heavy rain upstream. Slow to build, so forecasts can warn in advance. Common on the Brahmaputra, Ganga and Kosi.

⚡Flash flood

Very heavy rain or a cloudburst in hills sends water down valleys within minutes to hours. Hardest to warn for.

🌀Coastal & cyclone

Storm surge and cyclone rain push the sea inland along the east and west coasts.

🏙️Urban flood

Concrete, blocked drains and heavy rain flood cities such as Mumbai and Chennai.

🏔️Glacial lake outburst

A glacial lake in the Himalaya bursts and sends a surge down the valley.

🧱Dam & embankment

Failure, or sudden release of a full dam, floods areas downstream. This is where dam-level sensing helps.

How floods affect India

🏠Homes & displacementFamilies lose houses and are moved to relief camps, often for weeks.
🌾Farming & foodStanding crops, seed and livestock are lost, hitting farm incomes.
🛣️Roads & infrastructureRoads, bridges, power and phone lines fail, cutting villages off.
🩺HealthDirty water spreads diarrhoea, cholera, dengue and leptospirosis after floods.
🎓Education & livelihoodsSchools close, work stops and poor families take the longest to recover.
⏱️Why early warning mattersEven a few hours' notice lets people move, open gates and clear roads. That is Kavach's job.

Sources: NDMA flood-prone estimate and 2020 district count as reported by Down To Earth ↗. Descriptions of flood types and effects are general knowledge.

02 · THE ALGORITHM

Prediction, not just detection

Instead of one threshold alarm, each node combines three signals into a single risk score, then decides Safe, Warning or Danger.

FLOOD SCORE FORMULA

S = (L × 0.40) + (R × 0.35) + (Δ × 0.25)
💡 Hysteresis: the score must fall a small margin below a threshold before the mode steps down, which stops the alarm flickering near the boundary.
